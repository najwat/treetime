About TreeTime

TreeTime maps every in-service street tree in Seattle, loaded live from the City of Seattle's SDOT Trees data.

Diameter is the city's recorded trunk diameter in inches; values over 80″ are treated as not recorded. Park and private-yard trees aren't in this dataset.
